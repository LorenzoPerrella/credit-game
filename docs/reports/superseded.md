# Superseded reports

These three were generated by `creditsurv report` on **17 September 2026** and describe the model
as it stood then. They are grouped here, out of the sections whose current pages they used to sit
beside, so that nobody reads one by accident.

**What differs from the published model**, item by item: the covariates carry their old names
(`fico_s`, `cltv_drift`, `nfci_lagged`, `has_mi`); there is no HARP level, so about 8.6% of the
book and 135,070 defaults are outside the model entirely; estimation runs to 2024-12 rather than
2021-12, so there is no anchoring window and no out-of-time test window as rule 1 now declares
them; there is one backtest cut rather than three; the banded covariates are read as a line
through their midpoints rather than as bands, which rule 12 changed; and prepayment is independent
censoring rather than a competing risk.

**Why they are kept.** A superseded report is evidence of what was believed when, and this project
keeps retracted reasoning on the record rather than deleting it -- the same reason
[the register](../rules_register.md) exists and the same reason the withdrawn arguments stay in
[variable selection](../variable_selection.md) under a notice. Deleting them would make the
repository look as though it had always been right.

**Why they are not regenerated yet.** The second independent validation found them, and offered
the choice: regenerate or withdraw. Regenerating costs a full fit -- `creditsurv report` creates
its own cache entry -- of a specification that [the second round's plan](../second_round_plan.md)
is about to change in the macro block. Paying for that twice would be the same error in the other
direction. They are regenerated **once**, after the macro block lands, and this page goes with
them.

**Where the current numbers are**: [Calibration & backtest](../calibration.md) for the backtest and
the criteria, [the selection record](selection.md) for the specification,
[Windows, anchoring and grades](windows.md) for the three cuts and the master scale, and
[the fit engine's envelope](engine.md) for what any of it costs to reproduce.
